Stay Safe From Online Scams

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers trick users into sharing sensitive information.

🛡️ Phishing Safety Tips

🔗 Check Links

Check the website URL before clicking.

📧 Verify Emails

Verify emails from unknown senders.

🔐 Never Share OTP

Never share your OTP or password with anyone.

⚠️ Beware of Urgent Messages

Carefully verify links in urgent messages.

🛡️ Use Strong Security

Use strong passwords and enable MFA.

🚫 Report Suspicious Activity

Report suspicious emails or websites.
